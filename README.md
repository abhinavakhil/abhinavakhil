### Flagship projects

**✍️ [Penna](https://github.com/abhinavakhil/Penna)**: a rich text editor people actually enjoy using.
Notion-style editing, lossless Markdown, real tables and media, and free built-in AI. It's one small TypeScript package that works with React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Solid or plain HTML.
[Playground](https://penna.vercel.app/playground) · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@abhinavakhil/penna-core) · ![](https://img.shields.io/npm/v/@abhinavakhil/penna-core?style=flat-square&label=)

**🗺️ [GeoTag](https://github.com/abhinavakhil/GeoTag)**: every pile of waste, on the map. 🏆 *Category winner, Hack VSIT 3.0* ([certificate](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-HgYqYuM_fFLaa4FsJC1NLSKTGp51PTf/view?usp=sharing))
An AI-first PWA. You snap a photo and **on-device AI** sorts the waste into the right bin. The map gives every block a live waste score, and the worst spots get a ticket. There are **zero API keys**, and photos **never leave the device**.
[Live demo](https://geo-tag-pi.vercel.app) · React 19 + Vite
